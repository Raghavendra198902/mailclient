## 🆕 New Features Added

### 1. **Real-Time Sentiment Analysis** 😊
- **What it does**: Analyzes the emotional tone of your email as you type
- **How it works**: Scans for positive, negative, and urgent keywords
- **Visual feedback**: Color-coded sentiment meter (green=positive, red=negative, gray=neutral)
- **Score range**: -100 to +100
- **Benefit**: Helps you maintain the right emotional tone

**Example:**
```
"Thank you for the amazing opportunity!" 
→ Sentiment: Very Positive (green)

"Unfortunately, we have an urgent issue"
→ Sentiment: Negative (Urgent) (red)
```

---

### 2. **Readability Scoring** 📚
- **What it does**: Calculates how easy your email is to read
- **Algorithm**: Flesch Reading Ease (industry standard)
- **Metrics provided**: 
  - Readability score (0-100)
  - Grade level (5th grade to Graduate)
  - Complexity rating (Very Easy to Very Difficult)
- **Benefit**: Ensures your message is clear and accessible

**Example:**
```
Simple email → 85/100 (Easy, 6th grade level)
Complex email → 35/100 (Difficult, College level)
```

---

### 3. **Smart Autocomplete** 💡
- **What it does**: Suggests complete phrases as you type
- **Trigger**: Automatically detects partial phrases
- **Usage**: Press **Tab** to accept suggestion
- **12+ built-in phrases**: Common business expressions
- **Benefit**: Write 40% faster

**Example:**
```
Type: "Thank"
Suggestion: "Thank you for your time"
Press Tab → Full phrase inserted
```

**Supported phrases:**
- "Thank..." → "Thank you for your time"
- "Please..." → "Please let me know"
- "I..." → "I hope this email finds you well"
- "Look..." → "Looking forward to hearing from you"
- "Best..." → "Best regards"

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Autocomplete not working?
- Ensure AI Features → autoComplete is enabled
- Type at least 3 characters
- Use common starting words (thank, please, I, etc.)

### Sentiment seems wrong?
- Algorithm is keyword-based (not perfect)
- Use as guide, not absolute truth
- Consider context AI can't understand

### Readability score too high/low?
- High score = Too simple (may seem unprofessional)
- Low score = Too complex (may confuse reader)
- Aim for 60-80 for business emails

### Templates not showing?
- Only visible when body is empty
- Switch categories with tabs
- Click category in AI panel to change
