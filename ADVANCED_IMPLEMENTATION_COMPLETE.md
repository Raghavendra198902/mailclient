### 1. **Real-Time Sentiment Analysis** 😊📊
- Tracks emotional tone as you type
- Visual meter shows positive/negative/neutral
- Detects urgency keywords
- Color-coded feedback (green/gray/red)
- Helps maintain professional tone

### 2. **Readability Scoring** 📚
- Flesch Reading Ease algorithm
- Shows grade level (5th grade to Graduate)

- Helps write clearer emails

**Try it**: Write complex sentences and watch the score drop, simplify to see it rise

## 🎯 How to Use Everything

### Quick Workflow
```
1. Open /compose
2. AI panel opens automatically
3. Choose template category (Business/Personal/Support/Sales)
4. Click a template → AI generates content
5. Watch metrics update (sentiment, readability, word count)
6. Type more → autocomplete suggests phrases
7. Save versions before major changes
8. Click "Get Insights" for recommendations
9. Click "Schedule" or "Send Email"
```
